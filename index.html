// Solutions → Technology → TradeSpy

Know your supply chain
before it fails you.

Real-time exposure mapping for every NDC in your formulary. Country of origin. Manufacturer risk. Shortage probability. Domestic alternatives.

The Gap

FDA labels identify who markets a drug. Not who manufactures it.

The FDA labeler record is the U.S. marketing entity. The actual API manufacturing site may be in China, India, or elsewhere entirely. TradeSpy closes that gap with searchable, auditable intelligence built on real manufacturing data.

90% of active pharmaceutical ingredients used in U.S. drugs come from foreign manufacturers. TradeSpy maps exactly which ones — and where the risk concentrates.

  • Government and federal buyers (VA, DLA, DoD)
  • Compliance and regulatory affairs teams
  • GPO and formulary managers

// Company → Mission

Restore transparency, resiliency, and economic rationality to the
healthcare supply chain.

iRemedy was founded on a provable observation: the U.S. healthcare supply chain is structurally broken. Middlemen multiply. Prices are hidden. 90% of drug APIs come from foreign manufacturers — with a significant share representing a live national security risk.

We exist to fix that. Not through lobbying alone. Through infrastructure — direct distribution, AI-powered procurement intelligence, country-of-origin transparency, and compliance-native technology deployed at scale.

Made in America

The U.S. cannot sustain a healthcare system whose inputs are manufactured by adversaries. TradeSpy maps the exposure. The Incubator accelerates domestic manufacturers. iRemedy Direct creates the distribution channel. This is the infrastructure layer domestic reshoring requires.

Principles
01
Direct distribution where possible — fewer layers, lower cost, more accountability.
02
Technology as infrastructure, not software overhead — built to solve real operational problems.
03
Compliance-native from day one — built in, not bolted on.
04
Transparent economics — pricing should be visible; services must be profitable to be sustainable.
05
Healthcare supply chains are critical national security infrastructure — not just commercial systems.
